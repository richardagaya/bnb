01

What information do we collect?

Personal information you disclose to us

In short: we collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us.

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when you register on the Services, express interest in our products, participate in activities on the Services, or otherwise contact us. The personal information we collect may include:

  • Names
  • Email addresses
  • Passwords (stored securely by our authentication provider)
  • Profile photos you choose to upload
  • Contact preferences
  • Rental and business data you enter (bookings, expenses, property details, financial summaries)
  • Marketing email addresses submitted on our public site

We do not process sensitive information. If you register using a social media account, we collect certain profile information from that provider — see "How do we handle your social logins?" below.

All personal information you provide to us must be true, complete, and accurate, and you must notify us of any changes.

Information automatically collected

In short: some information — such as your IP address and device characteristics — is collected automatically when you use the Services.

This information does not reveal your specific identity but may include device and usage information used to maintain security, operate the Services, and support internal analytics. It includes:

  • Log and usage data — diagnostic, usage, and performance information our servers record automatically, including IP address, browser type, activity timestamps, and pages viewed.
  • Device data — information about the computer, phone, or tablet you use to access the Services, such as hardware model, operating system, and Internet service provider.
  • Location data — imprecise location inferred from your IP address for analytics and security. We do not collect precise GPS location from your device.

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What legal bases do we rely on?

In short: we only process your information when we have a valid legal reason to — consent, legal obligation, protecting vital interests, or legitimate business interests.

If you are located in the EU or UK

The GDPR and UK GDPR require us to explain our legal bases for processing:

  • Consent — where you've given permission for a specific purpose. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legitimate interests — where processing is reasonably necessary to achieve our business interests and doesn't outweigh your rights, such as analyzing and improving how the Services are used.
  • Legal obligations — where required to comply with law, cooperate with regulators, or defend our legal rights.
  • Vital interests — where necessary to protect someone's safety.

If you are located in Canada

We process information with your express or implied consent, which you may withdraw at any time. In limited cases permitted by law — such as fraud detection, business transactions, or compliance with a court order — we may process information without consent.

What are your privacy rights?

In short: depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict use of your information.

If you are in the EEA, UK, Switzerland, or Canada, you may have the right to access and obtain a copy of your data, request rectification or erasure, restrict processing, request data portability, and — where applicable — object to automated decision-making. Contact us via the details in "Contact us" to make a request.

If you are in the EEA or UK, you may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. If you are in Switzerland, you may contact the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner.

Withdrawing consent — where we rely on your consent, you may withdraw it at any time; this won't affect processing carried out before withdrawal.

Marketing communications — unsubscribe anytime via the link in our emails or by contacting us. We may still send necessary service-related messages.

Account information — review or update your account in your settings, or contact us to terminate it. We may retain limited information afterward to prevent fraud, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations.

11

Controls for Do-Not-Track features

No uniform technical standard for Do-Not-Track (DNT) signals currently exists, so we do not respond to browser DNT signals at this time. If a legal standard is adopted, we'll update this Notice accordingly.

Global Privacy Control (GPC) — we recognize and honor GPC signals. If your browser sends one, we treat it as a valid opt-out of the sale or sharing of your personal information for targeted advertising under applicable state laws, including the CCPA, applied automatically.
